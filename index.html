Investigation of electrons tunnelling through potential barriers

In this project, I investigated how electrons interact with different configurations of potential barriers. We start by examining how electrons interact with a single finite potential barrier, then move on to a potential step so on and so forth until we can model arbitrary number of potential barriers. Our main measures of interest are the transmission and reflection coefficients, calculated using the transfer and scattering matrices, as well as the amplitude of the wavefunction across the barriers. Below I show examples of these measures of interest, the transmission probability and wavefunction amplitude, for a double barrier system and finite barrier system respectively.
